No altitudes yet. Add the minimum altitudes from your approach chart.
  • Add the correction to the published minimum IFR altitude. Do not add it to an altitude assigned by ATC.
  • Published altitudes are entered as MSL. The correction is worked out on the height above the altimeter setting source, which is the aerodrome unless you set another elevation.
  • CAP GEN chart: the correction is interpolated between the chart's figures, in height and in temperature. The corrected altitude rounds up to the next 10 ft for an MDA/DA and to the next 100 ft for any other altitude. Above 5 000 ft the chart's 5 000 ft figure is used, plus the extra height at the rate between its 1 500 and 2 000 ft columns. The chart is in feet, so metres are converted.
  • The two AC 500-020 methods allow for the aerodrome elevation and the chart does not, so they give smaller corrections at high aerodromes. Accurate is valid to 36 000 ft. Simplified is within 5% of it for aerodromes up to 10 000 ft and heights up to 5 000 ft above them.
