No altitudes yet. Add the minimum altitudes from your approach chart.
  • Add the correction to the published minimum IFR altitude. Do not add it to an altitude assigned by ATC (§4.4).
  • Published altitudes are entered as MSL. The correction is worked out on the height above the altimeter setting source, which is the aerodrome unless you set another elevation.
  • Accurate is the iterative method and is valid to 36 000 ft. Simplified is within 5% of it for aerodrome elevations up to 10 000 ft and heights up to 5 000 ft above the aerodrome. Aerodrome pressure height is taken as the aerodrome elevation.
